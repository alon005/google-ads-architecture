# Google Ads architecture & conversion tracking

**13 years of building and running a US-wide Google Ads account for a call-center business, with conversion tracking that follows a click all the way to the profit.**

*Showcase page. Account details and scripts are private; a walkthrough is available on request.*

---

## The account

- **Built for Locksmith Connections**, a US locksmith dispatch brand I co-founded in 2013: people call, a call center books the job, and local independent technicians do the work.
- **Scale:** across 40+ states and 100+ cities. At its peak, about **9,500 campaigns** and **a few hundred thousand keywords**, one structure per market and service, later consolidated as Google moved advertisers to fewer, larger campaigns. The account spends several thousand dollars a day.
- **Architecture:** campaign and ad-group design per market and service, keyword and negative-keyword strategy, call assets and ad scheduling per market.
- **Compliance:** passed Google's Advanced Verification for locksmith advertisers, with every ad and landing page kept within its rules.

## Conversion tracking for a call center

- **A tracking number per market**, so every call from an ad is tied back to its campaign.
- **Offline conversions:** each finished job is uploaded back to Google with its real value, so Google bids on profit, not on clicks or calls.
- **Target ROAS on real job values**, with per-market economics deciding which areas open, scale or pause.

## The landing pages

- Built and ran the PPC landing pages and the organic site on WordPress for years, with the conversion tracking implemented by hand on every page.
- Rebuilt both on Cloudflare in 2026, with the tracking built in.

## Turnarounds

Led several turnarounds between 2023 and 2025 that kept the business from collapse, then rebuilt the account from the ground up in 2026 on real job and profit data.
